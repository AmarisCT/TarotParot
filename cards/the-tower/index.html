The Tower tarot card meaning

The Tower is card XVI of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Fire.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • sudden change
  • upheaval
  • revelation

A sudden shake-up clears what was built on shaky ground. It is unsettling, but it reveals the truth and makes room to rebuild.

Reversed

  • averted disaster
  • fear of change
  • delayed upheaval

You may be bracing against a change that needs to happen. Letting it come gently is easier than waiting for it to crash.
