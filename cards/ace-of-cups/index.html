Ace of Cups tarot card meaning

The Ace of Cups is the first card in the suit of Cups, the water suit of feelings and relationships. Its number carries the energy of new beginnings.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • new love
  • emotional opening
  • compassion

Your heart is opening. New love, friendship or creative feeling pours in.

Reversed

  • bottled feelings
  • emotional block
  • emptiness

Feelings are bottled up. Let yourself feel before you try to fix anything.
