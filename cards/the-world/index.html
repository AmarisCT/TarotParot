The World tarot card meaning

The World is card XXI of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Earth.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • completion
  • wholeness
  • accomplishment

A cycle is complete. Celebrate how far you have come; you are whole and ready for the next adventure.

Reversed

  • unfinished business
  • shortcuts
  • seeking closure

You are close, but something is left unfinished. Tie up the loose ends before you move on.
