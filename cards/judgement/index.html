Judgement tarot card meaning

Judgement is card XX of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Fire.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • awakening
  • reckoning
  • a calling

You are being called to rise. Reflect honestly on the past, forgive it, and answer the call toward who you are becoming.

Reversed

  • self-doubt
  • ignoring the call
  • harsh self-judgment

You are judging yourself too harshly, or ignoring an inner calling. Listen; it is still there.
