Strength tarot card meaning

Strength is card VIII of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Fire.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • courage
  • compassion
  • inner strength

Gentle strength wins this one. Patience, compassion and quiet confidence can tame what force never could.

Reversed

  • self-doubt
  • low energy
  • raw emotion

Self-doubt is louder than usual. Be kind to yourself and rebuild your confidence one small act at a time.
