King of Wands tarot card meaning

The King of Wands is the King in the suit of Wands, the fire suit of drive, creativity and ambition. Court cards often stand for a person, or a side of yourself you are being asked to step into.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • vision
  • leadership
  • boldness

Lead with vision. You see the big picture and can inspire others to follow.

Reversed

  • arrogance
  • impulsive leadership
  • impossible standards

Big plans need patience and humility. Do not let ego drive.
