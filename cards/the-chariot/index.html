The Chariot tarot card meaning

The Chariot is card VII of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Water.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • determination
  • momentum
  • victory

Pull your opposing impulses into one direction and drive. Willpower and focus carry you forward to a win.

Reversed

  • lack of direction
  • opposition
  • lost control

You are being pulled two ways, or pushing too hard against resistance. Take back the reins before you speed up.
