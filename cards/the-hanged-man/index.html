The Hanged Man tarot card meaning

The Hanged Man is card XII of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Water.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • pause
  • surrender
  • new perspective

Pause. Letting go of the need to act right now will show you the situation from a completely new angle.

Reversed

  • stalling
  • resistance
  • indecision

You are stalling, or resisting a sacrifice that needs to happen. Waiting longer will not make the choice easier.
