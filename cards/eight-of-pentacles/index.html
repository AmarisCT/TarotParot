Eight of Pentacles tarot card meaning

The Eight of Pentacles is the eighth card in the suit of Pentacles, the earth suit of money, work, body and home. Its number carries the energy of movement and mastery.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • skill
  • dedication
  • mastery

Put your head down and practice. Mastery comes through steady work.

Reversed

  • perfectionism
  • lack of focus
  • cutting corners

Perfectionism or distraction is slowing you down. Focus on the craft, not the flaws.
