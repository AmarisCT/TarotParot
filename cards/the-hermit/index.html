The Hermit tarot card meaning

The Hermit is card IX of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Earth.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • solitude
  • reflection
  • inner guidance

Step back from the noise. Time alone with your thoughts will light the next stretch of the path.

Reversed

  • isolation
  • loneliness
  • withdrawal

Solitude has turned into isolation. Reach out; you do not have to figure this out entirely by yourself.
