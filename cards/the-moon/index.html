The Moon tarot card meaning

The Moon is card XVIII of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Water.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • intuition

Not everything is as it appears. Move carefully, trust your instincts, and let dreams and feelings guide you through the uncertainty.

Reversed

  • clarity returning
  • released fear
  • lifting confusion

The fog is lifting. Fears that felt huge are shrinking, and the truth is coming into view.
