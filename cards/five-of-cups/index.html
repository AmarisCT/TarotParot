Five of Cups tarot card meaning

The Five of Cups is the fifth card in the suit of Cups, the water suit of feelings and relationships. Its number carries the energy of conflict and change.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • grief
  • loss
  • regret

Something was lost, and it hurts. But not everything spilled; look at what remains.

Reversed

  • acceptance
  • moving on
  • forgiveness

You are beginning to heal and move forward. Forgiveness, of yourself too, is near.
