The Fool tarot card meaning

The Fool is card 0 of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Air.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • new beginnings
  • faith
  • spontaneity

Something new is opening. Step toward it with curiosity and trust, even before you can see the whole road.

Reversed

  • recklessness
  • hesitation
  • fear of change

Either fear is keeping you at the edge, or you are about to jump without looking. Be brave, but stay awake.
