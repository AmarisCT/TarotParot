The Hierophant tarot card meaning

The Hierophant is card V of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Earth.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • tradition
  • guidance
  • shared beliefs

There is wisdom in the established way. A mentor, a tradition or a trusted institution can guide you now.

Reversed

  • rebellion
  • questioning norms
  • your own path

The usual rules do not fit you here. It may be time to question convention and find your own way of doing things.
