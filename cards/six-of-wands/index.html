Six of Wands tarot card meaning

The Six of Wands is the sixth card in the suit of Wands, the fire suit of drive, creativity and ambition. Its number carries the energy of harmony and healing.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • victory
  • recognition
  • confidence

Public success and well-earned recognition. Ride proudly.

Reversed

  • ego
  • fall from grace
  • going unnoticed

Recognition is not arriving, or pride is getting in the way. Your worth does not depend on applause.
