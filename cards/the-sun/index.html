The Sun tarot card meaning

The Sun is card XIX of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Fire.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • joy
  • success
  • vitality

Warmth, success and pure joy. This is one of the brightest cards in the deck: things are working out.

Reversed

  • passing clouds
  • dimmed optimism
  • overconfidence

The light is still there, just behind a cloud. Reconnect with simple joys, and do not let doubt or ego dim them.
