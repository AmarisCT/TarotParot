King of Cups tarot card meaning

The King of Cups is the King in the suit of Cups, the water suit of feelings and relationships. Court cards often stand for a person, or a side of yourself you are being asked to step into.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • emotional balance
  • diplomacy
  • calm

Stay calm and kind. Emotional maturity lets you steer through choppy water.

Reversed

  • emotional manipulation
  • moodiness
  • coldness

Emotions are being suppressed or used as leverage. Find your balance.
