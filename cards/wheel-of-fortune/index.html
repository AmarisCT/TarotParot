Wheel of Fortune tarot card meaning

Wheel of Fortune is card X of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Fire.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • cycles
  • fate
  • turning point

The wheel is turning. Change is coming whether you plan for it or not, and it is likely moving in your favor.

Reversed

  • resisting change
  • bad luck
  • repeating patterns

A cycle is repeating, or luck feels stuck. Notice the pattern; you can step out of it.
