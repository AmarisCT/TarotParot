The Empress tarot card meaning

The Empress is card III of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Earth.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • abundance
  • nurture
  • creativity

Growth comes through care. Nurture what you love, including your body and your creative work, and let it flourish at its own pace.

Reversed

  • smothering
  • creative block
  • self-neglect

You may be giving so much that you have run dry, or holding on too tightly. Tend to yourself first.
