Nine of Cups tarot card meaning

The Nine of Cups is the ninth card in the suit of Cups, the water suit of feelings and relationships. Its number carries the energy of nearing completion.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • contentment
  • wishes granted
  • satisfaction

The wish card. Contentment and satisfaction are yours; enjoy them.

Reversed

  • smugness
  • dissatisfaction
  • materialism

What you wished for did not bring the joy you expected. Look for what truly fulfills you.
