Seven of Swords tarot card meaning

The Seven of Swords is the seventh card in the suit of Swords, the air suit of thoughts, words and conflict. Its number carries the energy of reflection and assessment.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

Strategy is needed, but watch for deception, yours or someone else's.

Reversed

  • confession
  • getting caught
  • conscience

Secrets are surfacing. Come clean, or notice who is being less than honest.
