The Lovers tarot card meaning

The Lovers is card VI of the Major Arcana, the 22 cards that mark big life lessons and turning points. It is linked to the element of Air.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • love
  • alignment
  • meaningful choice

A bond or a choice asks for your whole heart. Choose what truly aligns with your values, and the connection deepens.

Reversed

  • disharmony
  • misaligned values
  • avoidance

Something is out of balance, in a relationship or within yourself. Be honest about what you actually want.
