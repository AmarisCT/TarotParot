Two of Pentacles tarot card meaning

The Two of Pentacles is the second card in the suit of Pentacles, the earth suit of money, work, body and home. Its number carries the energy of balance and choices.

Ask TarotParrot a question

Upright

  • balance
  • adaptability
  • juggling priorities

You are juggling a lot, and doing it well. Stay flexible.

Reversed

  • overwhelm
  • disorganization
  • overcommitment

Too many balls in the air. Drop the ones that do not matter.
